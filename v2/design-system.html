Design System

One confident blue, a warm paper ground and ink-drawn illustration. Quiet, editorial and a little unexpected, like the leaders we find.

  1. One bold thingThe illustration carries personality. Everything else stays calm.
  2. Flat, not floatingNo shadows. Tint and hairlines separate surfaces.
  3. Few sizesLarge type set light and tight; generous space between.
