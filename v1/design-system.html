How it behaves

Maroon card, 1 px maroon 850 border, 4 px radius. Number in List display (serif 300, 32), title in Heading 3 (serif 400, 26), then a 4 : 3 photograph.

On hover or focus a cream fill rises from the bottom over 560 ms (ease in-out). Number and title turn maroon as it passes, then the description writes in word by word in accent #7A1E5A at Body size, 28 ms apart.

Leaving reverses faster: the fill drops in 320 ms. Cards sit four across, auto-fit with a 240 px minimum; on phones the number drops to 24.

How it behaves

Cream panel, 4 px radius, 48 px padding (24 on phones), max 760 wide. Two columns, one on phones, where the button goes full width.

Labels sit above the field in 14 px Medium, ink 600. Fields are a 1 px cream 400 bottom rule with 15 px text; on focus the rule turns accent and thickens to 2 px (shown on work email).

On send, the form is replaced by the thank-you state, which fades up 8 px over 400 ms.

Motion

Two curves, short durations, and every entrance plays once. Motion should feel settled, never busy.

Rules

Hover effects only on (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine). Touch screens never get stuck hover states.

Tap and keyboard focus mirror hover, so nothing is mouse-only.

Exits are faster than entrances.

With prefers-reduced-motion, fades and colour stay; movement, blur, zoom and the count-up go.
