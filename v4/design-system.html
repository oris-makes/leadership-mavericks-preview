Brand system, version 2.0

Design System

One bold signal orange, deep charcoal and a warm paper ground, with ink-drawn illustration. Quiet, editorial and a little unexpected, like the leaders we find.

  1. One bold thingThe illustration carries personality. Everything else stays calm.
  2. Flat, not floatingNo shadows. Tint and hairlines separate surfaces.
  3. Few sizesLarge type set light and tight; generous space between.

06Motion

Quick to answer, calm to arrive.

Motion confirms a touch and paces the scroll. It never decorates: things fade up into place once, controls answer within 200ms, and nothing loops except the hero illustration’s slow float.

Hover needs a pointerHover effects only apply under (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine), so taps never leave sticky states.
Transitions, not keyframesInteractive states use transitions so they can be interrupted midway. Keyframes are kept for one-off entrances and the float.
Exits are fasterLeaving takes less time than arriving. Closing a step or the menu is near-instant.
Reduced motionWith prefers-reduced-motion, fades stay but movement goes: no rises, slides, scaling, drawing, float or count-up.
